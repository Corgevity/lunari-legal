Lunari Plus

US plans are $4.99 per month or $39.99 per year. Google Play shows the final price and terms before you confirm. Membership includes 90 new stories per month (up to approximately 30 minutes each) and 30 Surprise Me suggestions per UTC day. Annual membership has the same monthly allowance. Unused allowance does not roll over; regenerating a story counts as another story. Actual narration length depends on voice and speed.

Trial and renewal

Eligible new subscribers may receive one seven-day free trial, including 30 stories total and 10 suggestions per UTC day. After the trial, the chosen plan automatically renews at its displayed price unless canceled. Google Play determines eligibility and checkout terms. Manage or cancel in Google Play; cancellation ordinarily preserves access through the current paid period. Refunds follow Google Play’s applicable policy and law. Account deletion or uninstalling does not cancel billing.

Free features

Saved-story playback, downloaded voices, ambience, sleep controls and the journal remain free. Local data does not automatically move between devices. Availability of new generation depends on membership, connectivity and service availability; failed generation does not consume a successful-story allowance.

Wellbeing and content

Lunari is a creative relaxation app, not a medical service. It does not detect sleep stages or REM and does not guarantee sleep improvement or lucid dreams. Awareness cues are optional timed reminders. Keep volume comfortable and turn cues off if disruptive. AI may produce inaccurate or unsuitable content despite safeguards. Report concerning stories from the reader. Child-friendly mode adds safeguards but is not a guarantee; a responsible adult should review content for a child.
