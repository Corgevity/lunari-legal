Limited anti-abuse retention

After processing, a pseudonymous closure marker, minimal recent allowance counters and billing-period anchor remain for up to 90 days plus cleanup delay. These contain no story or journal text, raw account identifier or purchase token. This prevents allowance resets after deletion. Unfinished deletion remains blocked for support resolution. Reopening requires fresh Google sign-in and an explicit action; it does not buy or cancel a subscription.

Request without installing Lunari

Email support@corgevity.com from the Google email you used in Lunari. Use the subject “Delete Lunari account”. Say that you want your Lunari account and associated server data deleted. Do not send a password, payment details, story or journal text. We may ask you to verify account ownership. You may also use the in-app Privacy, terms & support → Request account deletion flow.

What happens next

Support verifies ownership and processes your request. Sending a request is not immediate deletion. It covers Lunari membership links, encrypted retry results, reports and other account-associated server records. Another app’s data and your Google account are preserved. Records required for payment, law or fraud prevention may be retained; support will explain the reason and retention period when applicable.

Subscriptions and local files

Cancel renewal separately in Google Play. Deleting an account or uninstalling Lunari does not cancel a subscription. Stories and journals stored on your device are not cloud-synced and are not remotely erased. Delete them in the app or clear Lunari app storage when you are ready; first export anything you want to keep.
