After account deletion

Once identity is verified, support removes Lunari account-associated server records. A pseudonymous closure marker, minimal recent allowance counters and a billing-period anchor are retained for up to 90 days plus database cleanup delay to prevent abuse and allowance resets. They contain no story or journal text, raw account identifier or purchase token. Unfinished deletion remains blocked until support resolves it. Reopening requires an explicit action and fresh Google sign-in; it does not buy or cancel a subscription. Shared Google/Firebase identity and other apps are preserved.

Your device

Dream drafts, saved stories, settings, voice preferences and your journal stay in app-local storage. They are not cloud-synced. Local records have no separate app password or database encryption; protect your phone with its screen lock. Android app backup is disabled. Exported backups use the password you choose. Uninstalling or clearing app storage can remove local records.

Story creation

Creating a story sends your dream-builder entries, requested length and child-friendly setting to our secure backend and OpenAI. Surprise Me sends its previous suggestion and child-friendly setting. Avoid entering identifying or sensitive information. We request that OpenAI not store responses; provider security and abuse-retention practices may still apply. Local narration and ambience do not upload story text.

Account and purchases

Google sign-in provides an account identifier and basic profile information through Firebase Authentication. Google Play processes payments. Our backend keeps encrypted purchase tokens, entitlement state, allowance counters and request identifiers to verify access and prevent abuse. It does not receive your card number. Firebase, Google Play and OpenAI process the information needed to provide these features; the app does not include advertising or analytics.

Retention

Encrypted story retry results are usable for 15 minutes; operation and request-rate records have short retention periods. Expiry prevents use immediately, while physical database cleanup can occur later. Purchase and allowance records remain while needed for membership, fraud prevention and legal obligations. A story report includes only the text and comments you explicitly agree to send; report text is retained for up to 30 days plus cleanup delay. Deletion requests are retained until processed and their resolution recorded.

Your choices

Local listening, voices, ambience, sleep controls and the journal do not require a subscription. You may sign out, delete individual saved content, or request deletion of your Lunari account below. A deletion request concerns Lunari server data, not another app using the same Google/Firebase identity. Local files remain until you delete them or clear this app’s storage. Account deletion does not cancel Google Play subscriptions; cancel in Google Play separately.

Contact

For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, email support@corgevity.com with the subject “Lunari privacy”. Do not send passwords, payment details or private dream/journal text. For an account-deletion request, contact us from the Google email used in Lunari; we may verify ownership before processing.
